Accolade badges — design proposal

Text badges in BarMagazine's own type, never third-party logos. Every badge is year-stamped, which is what makes it permanently true: a bar that was No. 32 in 2025 is always No. 32 in 2025, even after the 2026 list lands.

4 · The rules that keep it honest

Every badge carries a source URL in the data. If we can't cite it, it doesn't render — that's the whole accuracy guarantee, and it's auditable later.
Every badge carries its year. Nothing ever goes stale; it just becomes history.
Accolades are editorial, not commercial. They show on free listings exactly as on paid ones — which is what makes them worth having.
No third-party logos. 50 Best and BCA license their marks to listed venues, not to directories. Our own type is safer and looks more coherent than a row of mismatched logos.
