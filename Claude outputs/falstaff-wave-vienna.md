## dino-s-apothecary-bar-vienna
name: Dino's Apothecary Bar
city: Vienna
country: Austria
address: Salzgries 19, 1010 Vienna
website: https://www.dinos.at
type: Cocktail Bar
description: Heinz Kaiser's bar on Salzgries in the first district, staged as an apothecary and worked like one: infusions, tinctures and a long shelf of house preparations behind a counter that treats a drink as something compounded rather than poured. Open Tuesday to Saturday from five.

## d-bar-vienna
name: D-Bar
city: Vienna
country: Austria
address: Schubertring 5-7, 1010 Vienna
website: https://www.ritzcarlton.com/en/hotels/vierz-the-ritz-carlton-vienna/dining/

## bar-pani-vienna
name: Bar Pani
city: Vienna
country: Austria
address: Rossauer Laende 41, 1090 Vienna
website: https://www.barpani.com
type: Cocktail Bar
description: Classic and contemporary cocktails, Sardinian bar food and a record collection, on the Rossauer Laende by the Danube canal. The house describes itself as real cocktails and no nonsense, and the short hours back that up: Wednesday and Thursday from five, Friday and Saturday from six.
