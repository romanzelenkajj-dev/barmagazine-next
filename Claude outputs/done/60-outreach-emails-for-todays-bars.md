# Email addresses for everything added this week, ready for next week's batches

242 bars have gone in since Sunday. **166 carry an email, 76 do not.** Roman wants all of them ready to send next week, so this task is about closing that gap and staging the batches, not about sending.

## 1. Find the 76 missing addresses

Bar's own website contact page first, then its Instagram bio, then a booking or events page on its own domain. A venue-specific inbox on an operator or group domain counts. A generic agency or PR inbox does not, and a bar managed by a PR agency is parked rather than emailed, per the standing rule.

Anything you cannot find from the bar's own channels stays without an email. Do not guess a pattern like info@ from the domain.

## 2. Check the 166 you already have

Run them against `outreach/sent-log.txt`, `optout.txt` and `parked.txt` so nothing gets a second first-contact email, and drop any bar that has since claimed its profile.

## 3. Stage the batches, do not send

Build the slug lists as batch files in `outreach/`, split by region so each wave lands in the morning local time, the way batches 14 through 16 were built.

**Four batches, Monday through Thursday, one per day.** Roman's call, and the send log backs it. Of everything we have sent: Thursday 221, Tuesday 169, Friday 78, Wednesday 70, Monday 38. The "Wednesday is our best day at 17%" figure rests on 70 sends and twelve claims, which is far too small to plan around, and Monday is thinner still. Spreading Monday to Thursday fills the two thin cells and gives a real comparison by the end of the week instead of another lopsided sample.

Size the four batches roughly evenly rather than front-loading, or the comparison is worthless again. Keep each region landing in its own local morning.

Write the send commands out in the report, ready to run. **Send nothing.** Sends wait for Roman's confirmation in your own chat, and he decides the days.
