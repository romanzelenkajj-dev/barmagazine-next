# Opening hours: let Roman fix them, then stop owners typing them at all

Prophecy submitted hours as "Sunday to Thursday — 4:00pm to 1:00am Friday & Saturday — 4:00pm to 2:00am". Two em dashes, a format that matches nothing else in the directory, and no way to correct it: the review screen offers Approve & publish or Reject and nothing in between.

Roman: "There is no way for me to get rid of the M dashes or how do we make it so that all the hours are in the same format."

## The state of the data

`opening_hours` is a free-text string. 1,061 of 1,470 active bars have one and every single one is a string. The formats vary: "Mon-Thu 5pm-12am, Fri-Sat 5pm-1am", "Mon-Wed 16:00-01:00; Thu-Sat 16:00-02:00; Sun 15:00-00:00", "Thu 5pm-10pm; Fri-Sat 5pm-12am; Closed Sun-Wed". Two carry em dashes already: Isa Restaurant & Cocktail Bar and Punch Room.

`format-hours.ts` converts time tokens at render time per country and says in its own header that storage is never rewritten. So nothing normalises what an owner types.

## 1. Edit before approve, for every field

Task 66 built this for descriptions. Extend it to every field on a submission, hours included. Roman edits the proposed value in place, then approves. The owner's original stays on the submission row untouched.

This is the part he needs today. Do it first and it can ship on its own.

## 2. Structured hours in the owner form

Stop asking owners to type hours. Seven rows, one per day, each with an open time, a close time, and a closed toggle. A close time earlier than the open time means it runs past midnight, which is most of our bars, so handle that rather than rejecting it.

Store the structured value in a new column and generate the display string from it. The existing free-text `opening_hours` stays as the fallback for the 1,061 bars that have one, so nothing breaks and no migration is forced. A bar that submits structured hours gets its string generated by us, identically every time, and no owner can type a dash because there is nowhere to type one.

Do not migrate the existing 1,061 strings in this task. Report how many parse cleanly into the structured shape and how many would need a human, so Roman can decide whether that is worth a later pass.

## 3. What this unlocks, and why it is worth more than tidiness

Structured hours make three things possible that free text cannot: "Open now" on a card, "open late" as a filter, and correct `openingHoursSpecification` in the schema markup Google reads. For a directory where a large share of visits are someone deciding where to go tonight, that is a real feature, not housekeeping.

Build the column and the form in this task. Do not build the filters or the schema change yet, but say in the report what each would take.

## 4. The two live em dashes

## 5. An Archive button, because Reject is the wrong record

Roman: owners often send several photos and he wants one of them. Approving one and rejecting the rest marks good photos as rejected, which is both wrong in the record and, on the owner's dashboard, reads as a judgement on their work.

Rejecting sends no email. `src/app/api/admin/submissions/route.ts` sets `status: 'rejected'` and stops, so no owner has ever been notified. The concern is the record and what the owner sees on their own dashboard, not an email.

The status already exists. `scripts/archive-status-migration.sql` added `archived` to the check constraint with its reasoning written out: quietly shelved by an admin, no publish, no reject flow, no owner notification, `submitted_data` kept intact, hidden from the owner API, shown under the Archived tab. The tab is already in the admin filter row. What is missing is the action and the button.

Add `action === 'archive'` to the route, setting `status: 'archived'`, and an Archive button on the submission card beside Approve and Reject. Reject stays, for a submission that is actually wrong.

Archived rows must not appear to the owner as rejected, or at all. Check what the owner dashboard shows for each status and report it.

The photos on an archived row stay in `submitted_data`, so a photo passed over today can still be published later. Say in the report whether the admin screen lets Roman publish a photo from an archived row without the owner resubmitting, and if not, what that would take.
