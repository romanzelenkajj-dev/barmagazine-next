
## Two more things in the same template, found while checking send volume

**A postal address in the footer.** The template has no physical mailing address. US commercial email is expected to carry one, and we are emailing a lot of US bars. Spam filters also look for it, so its absence costs deliverability as well. Roman has given the exact wording. Put it in the footer small print, styled like the existing footer line, on one line or two:

BarMagazine LLC, [US address withdrawn: use the Slovak publisher address instead]
for PRO PUBLISHING s.r.o., Landererova 6, 811 09 Bratislava

The US entity leads because it sends the email and the listing is its service, and most recipients are American. The Slovak publisher is named so European recipients can identify who is behind the site. Do not reformat or abbreviate either address.

**A one-click unsubscribe.** Today the only opt-out is "reply 'unsubscribe'". That is an opt-out, but a reply costs the recipient effort, and a recipient who cannot opt out easily hits the spam button instead. Spam complaints are what actually damage a sending domain. Add a plain unsubscribe link in the footer, and add a List-Unsubscribe header on the Resend send.

Both are small and both protect the thing volume alone will not: the domain's reputation. Still no sending without Roman's confirmation.
