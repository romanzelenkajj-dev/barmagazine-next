# Task 128b: photo policy applied, sweep of every photo Code placed since 20 September

Date: 2026-09-24. Revised the same day after Roman's clarification: licensed photos stay.

## The rule as it now stands (in SETUP.md)

Code may take one photo from a bar's own website or Instagram, interiors only (the room, or the counter with its seating, as a guest sees it), never from Falstaff, Google, press or third-party sites, credited "Photo: <Bar name>" inside the hero. Licensed sources are a separate thing and are allowed: owner uploads, PR-agency photos, and The 50 Best Bars media pack used with the agency's permission and credited "Photo courtesy of The 50 Best Bars". What the rule forbids is Code taking an image from a third-party site on its own initiative. A licensed photo is never removed for failing the own-site test. Before publishing anything it scrapes, Code stages candidates in `outreach/photos-staged/<slug>/`, cropped to the hero ratio, and lists them as a contact sheet for approval.
