## Scope

155 active profiles with no photo in the 11 largest best-bars cities by photo-less qualifying bars: New York 21, Denver 15, London 15, Miami 15, New Orleans 14, Seattle 14, Berlin 13, Boston 12, Chicago 12, Las Vegas 12, Louisville 12. Only bars that qualify for their city's best-bars page and have a website.

## Audit applied before the sheet (same as 134a)

1. Original downloaded from the bar's or hotel's own site only; press or media-kit pages searched first.
2. EXIF, IPTC and XMP read on every file (Artist, Creator, Copyright, Credit, Source, By-line); file name checked; source page read for credits.
3. Any photographer, studio or agency named for the image, in the file name, on the page, or across the site's own images: rejected. Where a site credits a photographer on other images of the same shoot or page, the uncredited ones were held back too.
4. Germany: press or media-kit page images only, Impressum read. Berlin yielded 1 (Hotel Adlon press room).
5. Every candidate re-checked by me with the audit script, then viewed at full size for watermarks, people as subject, and quality.
