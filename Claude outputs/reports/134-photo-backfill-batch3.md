## New lesson, and a re-check of what is live

Two groups found that a resized copy can hide a credit the original carries: Aldea's WordPress "-scaled" copy was clean while the full-size original names Facundo Ruiz; Lucia's Bento-resized copy was clean while the raw file names Brandon Barré. The brief now requires the original file, and I re-checked the originals behind every live photo that came from a resized copy: Angelina, Bidder Bar, Dom Whisky Gdańsk and Wrocław, Solaire Rooftop and Roquette are clean at full size. Smile Tree's original redirects away and two Sanity-hosted files (Semiprecious, Starlite) refuse the unresized request, so those rest on the copies checked at the time. Squarespace, Wix and Webflow URLs were already the original uploads.
