## Why the two shortfalls are real

- **Oslo.** Falstaff lists eight Oslo cocktail bars we did not have. Harlem, Torggata Botaniske
  and Bettola are in. Anam Cara is on HOLD in the wave file: Falstaff gives Rostockgata 42, but the
  bar's own site (a Squarespace template still titled "Your Site Title") and its Instagram bio carry
  no street. About Contrasts calls itself coffee, cocktails and culture, a day cafe first. Den
  Grimme Ælling shares Svanen's address and row. Vogue Scandinavia's October 2025 six also named
  Dangerous Club and Dæl: Dæl closed in 2023, and Dangerous Club closed its Maridalsveien room in
  April 2024 (Avisa Oslo); its domain now describes a members' music club at another address. 50
  Best Discovery's other Oslo entries (Hot Shop, Kontrast) are restaurants. Say the word and Anam
  Cara goes in on Falstaff's line with the flag, which lifts Oslo to ten.
- **Kraków.** No Falstaff coverage; Discovery has only Tag (in) and Bottiglieria (a restaurant).
  SIX RoofTop is on HOLD: In Your Pocket gives Św. Stanisława 10, but the bar has no website and
  its Instagram bio carries hours and no street. Rumour publishes its own address (Pijarska 9) but
  surfaced on no list I could cite, so it is not in. Lastriko calls itself a cocktail bar and bistro
  with a weekend cellar club and its Instagram profile is unavailable. Skwer Bar and Labour (from
  the BCA watchlist) still publish no address on their own channels. Sky Bar at Hotel Stary is a
  seasonal grill and bar. Pergamin is a chill-out music bar; Alchemia, Singer and the Kazimierz
  rooms on Fodor's and Continents & Condiments are pubs, dives or beer bars.

## Judgment calls

- Harry's Bar is listed by Discovery as a restaurant and by Cipriani's schema as one; it is
  admitted as Classic Bar + Restaurant Bar because it is Harry's Bar. Reverse it if you disagree.
- The Devil's Advocate calls itself a whisky bar and restaurant, whisky bar first; admitted as
  Whisky Bar + Cocktail Bar on Time Out's cocktail-bar listing.
- Gin Mill has no website; the row's website field points at its Instagram, which is where its
  address and the whole of its own description (tiny gin heaven, nine seats, 150 gin bottles,
  walk-in only) live. No hours published.
- Dragonfly, Movida and Mr. Black publish no opening hours on their own channels; the hours
  fields are empty rather than borrowed from In Your Pocket.
- Torggata Botaniske's site and Instagram bio disagree on closing times (site: one and two;
  Instagram: one and three); the site's hours are stored.
- Bar Longhi's hours are April to October only, and the row says so.
- Excluded on their own words: Little Capo (Italian restaurant, despite a Spirited 2026 restaurant
  bar honour), Crybaby (jukebox and chicken wing pub), Paradise Palms (bar, diner, label, club),
  The Voodoo Rooms (bar, restaurant and grassroots music venue), 1820 Rooftop (Johnnie Walker
  Princes Street attraction, no own page found).
