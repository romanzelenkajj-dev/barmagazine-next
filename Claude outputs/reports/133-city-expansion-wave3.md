- **Third-party addresses.** Seven addresses came from third parties, not the bar's own channels: Silk and Fizz, Nebotičnik, 5L, Teoria, Negroni, Oficiul 1 and nosaints. I checked each one against the venue's Google Business profile, the standard you set for Founders, and all seven match. The source is recorded in address_source. Oficiul 1 and nosaints only hand their address to guests after booking, but both publish it themselves on Google.
- **Discovery labels.** Two 50 Best Discovery sources were labelled "(World's 50 Best)", so they counted as selective while the same kind of listing elsewhere did not. I relabelled them "The 50 Best Discovery" so all Discovery entries are treated the same. See your calls below.
- **Validator fixes.** Four rows were rewritten or held. Mayday's "two glass towers" matched the award pattern. Blaue Brigitte's excerpt had the word "spirited". Herrengedeck's menu section is called "Greatest Hit". Brick's (Leipzig) has only a Facebook page, last updated May 2025, so it is held.

## How the rows were built

The procedure and guards are the same as 130 and 131. Seven research agents covered the 19 cities and returned 138 candidates including spares. The 133 brief added your later rulings:
- Local-language best-of lists from established publications count as selective.
- Gault&Millau counts.
- A distillery's cocktail bar is admissible; taprooms and restaurant-style wine bars are not.
- Falstaff is research-only.

Every row has 2026 evidence that it is trading. Descriptions say only what the venue says about itself, and no award is named. Wave file: `Claude outputs/133-city-expansion-wave3-verified.md`. I ran `wave-insert.mjs` as a dry run first, then with `--apply`.

## Your calls

1. **50 Best Discovery.** It's a curated inclusion, not a ranked list. Right now no Discovery entry counts. Counting it would add Whisper Sister and Sigmund Freud (Tallinn), Gimlet (Riga), Silk and Fizz (Ljubljana) and Sputnik (Sofia).
2. **Ljubljana Times**, "5 najboljših koktajl barov v Ljubljani" (2025), is a daily city news portal. If it counts as established, Kolibri, Silk and Fizz, B-Bar, Champagne Suzy and Captain's Cabin qualify, and Ljubljana becomes "The 6 Best Bars in Ljubljana".
3. **Neighborhood.lv**, "Top 14 Bars" (Riga, 2023), is a Riga lifestyle site. It currently counts, because English-titled lists are judged on the title alone. It backs Gimlet, Herbary, Mākonis and nosaints.
4. **Secret Media city guides** (Sevilla Secreta, Valencia Secreta). These are the same network as Guadalajara Secreta, so they don't count. Counting them would give Seville and Valencia numbered titles.
5. **1000things**, "Die 8 besten Bars in Graz". This is also a digital city guide, so it doesn't count. Graz is already "The 6 Best" without it.
6. **Borderline venues:**
   - Nebotičnik (Ljubljana) is a café from 09:00 and a rooftop bar at night.
   - Nomad Skybar (Bucharest) is restaurant, bar and club in one.
   - Ärkel (Tallinn) has hookah on its menu.
   - The Blue Cup and Imperial Rooftop (Thessaloniki) are all-day venues.
   - NY Basement (Rotterdam) is the Hotel New York's bar-restaurant, built around an 11 metre cocktail bar.
   - Katze Katze (Graz) turns into a club late at night.
7. **Göteborg vs Gothenburg.** The existing row uses "Göteborg", so the page is /best-bars/goteborg. The rest of the site uses English names. Renaming it would need a 301 from the old address.
